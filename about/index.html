ABOUT AI KEEPER

Independent explainers, worked examples and tools for understanding AI infrastructure, costs and practical use.

Who this is for.
How we work.

A model is one layer. Its useful operation depends on facilities, electricity, equipment, capital and a business process that turns output into value.

AI Keeper connects those layers. We write for founders, operators, investors and curious readers who want to understand the dependencies behind an announcement and the assumptions behind a forecast.

AI Keeper is published under the project name. Materials combine explanations, source-based analysis and clearly labelled teaching examples. We link factual claims to public evidence, show calculation assumptions and invite corrections. AI-generated illustrations are conceptual; they do not document a named project. Start with a guide, test an assumption with a tool, then follow the supporting sources.

THE EDITORIAL COMMITMENT

Evidence first.
Interpretation made explicit.
Uncertainty kept in view.

Depth over velocity.

This edition begins with explainers, analysis and practical frameworks. It is not a live market-data service or a stream of model announcements.

We separate reported evidence, our interpretation and constructed examples. We do not use invented customers, testimonials or performance results to make an argument feel stronger.
