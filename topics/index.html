THE FIVE LENSES

AI is a system.
Choose a way in.

These are connected perspectives, not isolated beats. Follow a topic, then look at the dependencies around it.

01

Infrastructure

Trace the chain from equipment to a working service. Capacity matters when it can support the workload a customer actually needs.

↗02

Energy

Read the power offer, define the capacity boundary and connect energy costs to the service being sold.

↗03

Economics

Separate revenue, contribution and cash requirements. A capacity announcement or a large funding number cannot establish the economics of delivery.

↗04

Geopolitics

Understand where a system operates, who controls each layer and which assumptions about access need to be reviewed.

↗05

Practical AI

Start with an accepted customer outcome. Compare quality, human review and total task cost before expanding the commitment.
