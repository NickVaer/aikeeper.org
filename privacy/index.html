This page describes the behavior of this edition of the AI Keeper website.

Browsing the site

The articles, resources and calculator are available without an account. There is no contact form or email subscription form. Email and X links take you to those services, whose own terms and privacy practices apply.

Optional Google Analytics

On aikeeper.org and www.aikeeper.org, this edition loads Google Analytics only after you choose Allow analytics. The measurement ID is G-VQT6BQJ7EG. Analytics can collect information about visits, pages and interactions and can use cookies. With your consent, we also record clicks on marked X profile links and their location on the site. This measures profile visits initiated here, not whether you follow the account. Blocking scripts or declining analytics can prevent a visit from appearing in reports.

Your choice is stored in this browser's local storage. You can reopen the controls through Analytics preferences in the footer. Declining stops this edition from loading analytics on subsequent page loads; previously collected information is not deleted by that choice. If analytics was already active, the page reloads to stop its scripts. Browser controls can remove existing cookies and local storage.

The calculator

Calculations run in your browser. Inputs are not sent to an AI Keeper application server by the calculator. Scenario downloads are created locally. The page URL and visit may still be included in analytics if you allow it; calculator inputs are not intentionally included in analytics events.

Hosting and contact

Requests necessarily reach the hosting infrastructure, which may retain access or operational logs according to its configuration. Email you send is processed by the email service. Do not send confidential project documents in an initial enquiry.

Questions

For questions about this site, email aikeeper.io@gmail.com.

This describes the delivered site behavior; it is not a representation of independently audited hosting retention settings.
